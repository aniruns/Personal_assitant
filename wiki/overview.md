## What this KB is

A personal second brain maintained by an LLM agent following the [[llm-wiki-pattern]]. Raw
sources go in `raw/`, the agent compiles them into the pages under `wiki/`, and `CLAUDE.md` is
the schema. Started 2026-09-17.
