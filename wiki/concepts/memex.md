# Memex

[[vannevar-bush]]'s 1945 proposal for a personal, curated knowledge store in which documents are
connected by "associative trails". Cited by the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] as its intellectual ancestor.

## Explanation

As described in the LLM Wiki source, the Memex was *private* and *actively curated*, and its
central value was the connections between documents, not just the documents. The source argues
this was closer to what a personal wiki is than to what the web became (public, un-curated),
and that the piece Bush couldn't solve was **who does the maintenance** of the trails — which an
LLM agent can now do ([[llm-wiki-idea-file|source]]).

**Assessment:** the KB currently knows the Memex only second-hand through this one source.
Bush's essay ("As We May Think", *The Atlantic*, 1945) would be the primary source to ingest.
