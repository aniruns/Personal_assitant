# Vannevar Bush

American engineer and science administrator; proposed the [[memex]] in 1945. Appears in this KB
as the origin point of the "personal curated knowledge store with associative trails" idea that
the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] claims descent from.

## What we know

- Proposed the Memex in 1945 as a private, actively curated store where the links between
  documents are as valuable as the documents ([[llm-wiki-idea-file|source]]).
- Per the source, the problem his design left open was who maintains the trails.

The KB knows Bush only through this one second-hand mention; nothing here beyond the Memex
connection is sourced.
